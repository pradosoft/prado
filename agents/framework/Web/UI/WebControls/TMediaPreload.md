## Overview
The `preload` hint of a [TBaseMedia](./TBaseMedia.md).

| Constant | `preload` |
|---|---|
| `NotSet` | Not rendered; the browser decides (default) |
| `None` | `none`: nothing loads until play |
| `Metadata` | `metadata`: duration and dimensions |
| `Auto` | `auto`: the whole resource may load |

**@since 4.4.0**
